Add `OnboardingEmptyState`, a 50/50 editorial layout for first-time onboarding surfaces. It composes a slotted `Heading`, `Text`, and `ButtonGroup` of `LinkButton`s alongside a media gallery that shows a single image or an auto-advancing, keyboard-navigable carousel (built on `Tabs`, with light/dark image support and reduced-motion handling).
